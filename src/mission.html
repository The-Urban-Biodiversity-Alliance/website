Mission

We unite local activists across Malden, Medford, Melrose, and surrounding communities to drive meaningful environmental change through education, collaboration, and action. By connecting diverse groups, hosting impactful events, and celebrating collective achievements, we build a powerful network dedicated to biodiversity conservation, sustainable living practices, and environmental justice. Our work amplifies community voices, fosters innovative solutions, and empowers residents to create the healthy, resilient neighborhoods we all deserve.
