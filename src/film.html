Film Showings

We show films with an emphasis on new release films by local and underground filmmakers.

Inundation District: Film Showing and Filmmaker Talk with Filmmaker David Abel

Melrose UU Climate Action Team, Friends of NEMT Forest and The Urban Biodiversity Alliance hosted award winning filmmaker and Boston Globe writer David Abel for a showing of Inundation District (2023). This film is about climate change in Boston focusing on the rising tide in Boston's Seaport District by award winning filmmakers David Abel and Ted Blanco and the Boston Globe.

Learn more about the film at https://www.inundationdistrict.com/.

Thoughtful Thursday: A screening and discussion of the documentary short film Dukakis: Recipe for Democracy

Melrose Unitarian Universalist Church and The Urban Biodiversity Alliance hosted award winning filmmaker and producer Erin Trahan and Jeff Schmidt for a showing of of the documentary short film Dukakis: Recipe for Democracy. The 28-minute documentary catches up with Massachusetts’ longest-serving governor three decades after he ran for the presidency.

Learn more at https://www.dukakisfilm.com/.
