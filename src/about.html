About

The Urban Biodiversity Alliance serves the Malden, Medford, Melrose area and focuses on environmental action and biodiversity conservation through sustainable agriculture, permaculture, urban farming, and green technology initiatives. We work to amplify positive environmental victories and community efforts while supporting environmental justice and indigenous rights. Our goal is to build a network of local activists by creating meaningful discussions, connecting with other environmental groups, and keeping the community informed and engaged in creating positive environmental change in their local area.
